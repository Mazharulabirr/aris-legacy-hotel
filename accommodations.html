Aris Legacy  /  Rooms

Rooms

One hundred and twenty upscale rooms, eighty residences, and four suites on the second floor.

Dual brand

Two hundred rooms
behind one door.

Aris Legacy is planned as a dual-brand house: 120 upscale rooms for the night before a flight or a fixture, and 80 extended-stay residences for the season, the shoot, the trade and the relocation.

Guests are not meant to notice the seam. One lobby, one kitchen, one team, one standard — an arrangement that keeps service consistent and the operation lean enough to hold that standard in a quiet week.

The rooms

Four ways to stay.

Upscale · 120 rooms

The Legacy King

The room most guests meet first, and the one the hotel will be judged on. Floor-to-ceiling glazing, a desk that can actually be worked at, and blackout drapery specified to the standard airline crews need at four in the afternoon.

  • King Bed
  • Blackout to Crew Spec
  • Workstation
  • Rainfall Shower
  • Smart Controls

Upscale · Double queen

The Roster Room

Two queens for the travelling party — squads, crews, tour staff, families connecting through Atlanta. Identical finishes to the Legacy King with more floor, and blocked along single corridors so a group is not scattered across three storeys.

  • Two Queen Beds
  • Group Blocking
  • Extended Storage
  • Rainfall Shower

Extended stay · 80 residences

The Residence

Drawn for weeks rather than nights, and for the production crews and relocations that move through Atlanta constantly. A full kitchen, a separate living area, a proper workspace and laundry in the residence, with housekeeping on a weekly rhythm.

  • Full Kitchen
  • Separate Living
  • In-Residence Laundry
  • Weekly Service
  • Long-Stay Rates

Signature · 4 suites

The Presidential Suites

Four suites on the second floor, each fifty feet by forty, opening onto the terrace. Private dining for eight, a separate entrance for anyone travelling with a party, and a route to the screening room that never crosses a public corridor.

  • 50 ft × 40 ft
  • Private Terrace
  • Dining for Eight
  • Discreet Arrival
  • Screening Room Access

In every room

The standard,
not the upgrade.

A hotel two miles from a hub airport is judged on what works at unsociable hours, and a hotel built for athletes and production crews is judged on more than that. These are specified across all two hundred rooms rather than reserved for the top of the rate card.

High-density Wi-Fi

Engineered for full-floor occupancy and live uploads, not for a lobby speed test.

Cast-ready displays

Guests sign in to their own accounts and are signed out automatically at checkout.

Circadian lighting

Scenes tuned for arrival, work, wind-down and a four o'clock departure.

Acoustic separation

Party-wall and slab detailing carried through both brands, including the residences.

Airport transfer

Scheduled against the departure banks, on demand through the night.
