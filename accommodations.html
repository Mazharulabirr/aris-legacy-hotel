Rooms

One hundred and fifty upscale rooms, fifty residences, and four suites on the second floor.

Dual brand

Two hundred rooms
behind one door.

Aris Legacy is planned as a dual-brand house: 150 upscale rooms for the night before a flight or a fixture, and 50 extended-stay residences for the season, the shoot, the trade and the relocation.

Guests are not meant to notice the seam. One lobby, one kitchen, one team, one standard — an arrangement that keeps service consistent and the operation lean enough to hold that standard in a quiet week.

The rooms

Four ways to stay.

Upscale · 120 rooms

The Legacy KingStarting Lineup

The Legacy Rooms are the starting lineup of amenities designed for comfort and convenience, available in King and Double Queen configurations. Guests will enjoy premium bedding, spacious bathrooms, high-definition smart TVs, high-speed Wi-Fi, a dedicated workspace, in-room coffee and refreshments, luxury bath amenities, USB charging stations, and thoughtful touches that make every stay feel like a first-class experience.

  • King Bed
  • Blackout to Crew Spec
  • Workstation
  • Rainfall Shower
  • Smart Controls

Upscale · 30 rooms

VIP Legacy SuitesThe Champions

The VIP Legacy Suites are designed for champions who expect an elevated level of luxury, privacy, and service. These spacious suites will feature separate living and sleeping areas, premium designer furnishings, upgraded bathrooms with soaking tubs or luxury showers, enhanced entertainment systems, private dining areas, wet bars, upgraded bedding and linens, curated refreshments, and exclusive VIP services designed to make every stay feel like a victory celebration.

  • King Bed
  • Group Blocking
  • Extended Storage
  • Rainfall Shower

Extended stay · 50 residences

Legacy ResidencesThe All-Star Lineup

The Legacy Residences are designed for extended stays, combining the comforts of home with luxury hotel service. Ideal for athletes, production crew, entertainment professionals, and relocating executives, each residence features expanded living and sleeping areas, upgraded furnishings, premium bedding, larger bathrooms, a full kitchen, enhanced entertainment systems, dining and work spaces, additional storage, and weekly housekeeping — built for stays measured in weeks, not nights.

  • Full Kitchen
  • Separate Living
  • In-Residence Laundry
  • Weekly Service
  • Long-Stay Rates

Signature · 4 suites

Legacy Presidential SuitesHall of Fame

The Legacy Presidential Suites offer a Hall of Fame-caliber experience for the hotel’s most distinguished guests. These four 1,800-square-foot second-floor suites face the front of the hotel and open onto a private terrace. Each features expansive living and entertainment spaces, a luxury master bedroom, exceptional bathrooms, private dining for eight, premium bar and hospitality areas, advanced media systems, bespoke furnishings, and personalized concierge service. A private entrance and discreet, private route to the screening room provide exceptional privacy and exclusivity — delivering a refined luxury experience worthy of the Aris Legacy brand.

  • 1,800 SQFT
  • Private Terrace
  • Dining for Eight
  • Discreet Arrival
  • Screening Room Access

In every room

The standard,
not the upgrade.

A hotel two miles from a hub airport is judged on what works at unsociable hours, and a hotel built for athletes and production crews is judged on more than that. These are specified across all two hundred rooms rather than reserved for the top of the rate card.

High-density Wi-Fi

Engineered for full-floor occupancy and live uploads, not for a lobby speed test.

Cast-ready displays

Guests sign in to their own accounts and are signed out automatically at checkout.

Circadian lighting

Scenes tuned for arrival, work, wind-down and a four o'clock departure.

Acoustic separation

Party-wall and slab detailing carried through both brands, including the residences.

Airport transfer

Scheduled against the departure banks, on demand through the night.
