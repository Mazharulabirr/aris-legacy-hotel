The full vision

The Aris Legacy Hotel is an upcoming sports and media-themed hotel development, designed to create a new destination for athletes, entertainers, sports fans, media professionals, travellers and families visiting Atlanta.

It is envisioned as more than a traditional hotel — an integrated sports, media and entertainment experience where guests can stay, socialise, train, watch major sporting events, attend entertainment programming and connect with professionals from across the sports and entertainment industries.

A place where an athlete can stay before a major competition, where fans gather to experience a game, where entertainers take part in special events, where media professionals connect — and where a traveller finds something distinctly different from a conventional hotel. Not simply a place to sleep while visiting Atlanta.

The development case

The hotel sits on approximately five acres of prime real estate just two miles from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport — the busiest and most diverse airport in the world, moving more than a hundred million passengers a year, and the gateway to Atlanta's domestic and international transport network.

The surrounding five-mile area is a significant economic market in its own right, with an estimated $70 billion in annual economic activity cited as part of the project's development opportunity.

The site is being developed in a designated Opportunity Zone, combining hospitality, entertainment, economic development and investment in one strategically located destination.

What the property
is designed to hold.

The property is expected to carry a distinctive sports and entertainment atmosphere throughout the guest experience, creating a destination that appeals to visitors and to the local community alike. Potential features and programming may include:

  • Sports-themed hospitality and guest experiences
  • Entertainment and live-event programming
  • Sports viewing and social spaces
  • Media and content-related opportunities
  • Celebrity and athlete appearances
  • Meeting and event facilities
  • Hospitality experiences for sports teams and entertainment productions
  • Special events, tournaments and fan experiences
  • Restaurants, lounges and entertainment venues
  • Partnerships with professional and collegiate sports organisations
  • Opportunities for brands, sponsors and corporate partners

Experiences in detail

Our hospitality partner

IHG Hotels
& Resorts.

The Aris Legacy Hotel has selected IHG Hotels & Resorts as its hospitality partner. IHG has offered the property the opportunity to operate under its Noted Collection soft brand.

That gives the project access to the resources, systems, standards and global distribution capabilities of one of the world's leading hotel companies, while allowing the property to keep a distinctive identity of its own. The partnership is intended to combine the character of the Aris Legacy Hotel with the global hospitality infrastructure of IHG.

IHG's US headquarters are in Atlanta, which makes this site a natural fit for its continued growth in the airport market.

Interest in the project

The project has attracted interest from a number of individuals within the sports and entertainment communities. Several celebrities and high-profile personalities have expressed interest in potentially participating as brand ambassadors, strategic partners and investors.
